Table Kit

Tools for running a 5th edition game. They work in your browser with no sign-up.

Hero Ledger

Build a character under the 2014 or 2024 rules. Stats, attacks, spell slots and legality checks update as you go. Includes an Excel version.

Map Builder

Draw square or hex maps, generate dungeons, caves and overlands, hide areas under fog and show players a live second screen.

Story Manager

Keep sessions, quests, NPCs, places and secrets in one linked campaign notebook, with a timeline and relationship web.

Your work stays in this browser and is not uploaded. Clearing site data removes it, so use each tool's Export button to keep a copy or move it to another device.
